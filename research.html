Research

Epitranscriptomics and RNA modifications

Statistical and computational methods for RNA modification data, including m6A methylation and related sequencing technologies. Current interests include read-level detection, stoichiometry estimation, differential methylation, and RNA-modification dynamics.

Single-cell and spatial omics

Methods for single-cell and spatial transcriptomic data, including marker identification, spatially varying molecular signals, cell-type-specific inference, and integration across molecular layers.

Statistical learning for biomedical data

Statistically principled and computationally efficient models for high-dimensional biomedical data, with emphasis on uncertainty quantification, robust inference, representation learning, and biologically interpretable analysis.

Multi-omics integration

Integration of transcriptomic, epigenomic, and epitranscriptomic measurements to better understand molecular regulation and disease mechanisms.

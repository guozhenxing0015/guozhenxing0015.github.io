Bio

My research lies at the intersection of statistical methodology and computational biology. I develop statistical and computational methods for modern sequencing data, with a particular focus on RNA epigenetics and epitranscriptomics, transcriptomics, and single-cell and spatial omics. My methodological interests include inference for RNA modifications, differential analysis, representation learning, and statistical modeling of complex biological data.

Research in my lab has been generously supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) Young Scientists Fund (12401650, PI: Guo), the Young Scholar Projects under the 1+1+1 CUHK-CUHK(SZ)-GDSTC Joint Collaboration Fund (co-PI: Guo), and an NSFC Special Project (82441027, PI: Lu; Co-Investigator: Guo).

I am currently an Assistant Professor in the School of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. Before joining CUHK-SZ, I received my Ph.D. in Biostatistics from Emory University under the guidance of Prof. Hao Wu. Before that, I received my M.S. in Operational Research from the the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and my B.S. in Applied Mathematics from Hunan University.
